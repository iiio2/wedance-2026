As a community using a free/OpenAir commons (e.g. the Pinakothek Open Air space), we want to elect a moderator each year, so that the community — not a platform admin — chooses who stewards the space.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A free/OpenAir space can open a moderator election with a defined **open date and close date**; the winner serves a **one-year term**.
- **Only one election runs per space at a time** — a new election can't open while one is active.
- The election moves through clear, **timestamped phases**: nominations open → voting open → closed.
- Any dancer with a **real, verified profile** can **self-nominate** as a candidate before voting opens (no admin appointment).
- **Voter eligibility is enforced: one real profile = one vote** — duplicate or fake accounts cannot stack the ballot (ties to J7 — real people, no fakes). Casting mechanics live in G803.
- On close, the winning moderator is **linked to their real profile** and recorded against the space with the term's **start and end dates**.
- **Past election records are retained and viewable**, so the space's governance history is auditable across terms.

## Notes
Eligibility here is the anti-stuffing safeguard the open ledger (G803) relies on — timestamps make the count auditable, but only real-profile + one-vote makes the count *legitimate*. Guideline proposals attach in G802; the open vote runs in G803; the elected moderator's duties + per-term guideline versioning are G804.
