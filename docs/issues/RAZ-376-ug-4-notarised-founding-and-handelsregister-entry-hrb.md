# RAZ-376 — UG 4 · Notarised founding and Handelsregister entry (HRB)

**Tension.** A UG exists only after a notarised founding and the Handelsregister entry.

**Do (Commander):**

1. Pick a notary in Munich. Online founding by video is also possible for a UG founded with cash (since Aug 2022).
2. Send the notary the setup (RAZ-374): Musterprotokoll, Alex as sole shareholder and Geschäftsführer, name, seat, address, Stammkapital.
3. Sign at the appointment. Bring ID.
4. Pay the Stammkapital into the new business account (next issue). The notary needs the bank's confirmation before filing.
5. The notary files with the Handelsregister → **HRB number**. Until then, sign as "WeDance UG (haftungsbeschränkt) i.G.".

**Costs:** notary and register fees. Ask for a quote; the Musterprotokoll is the cheap route.

**Blocked by:** setup decision, OMMAX consent, name check.
