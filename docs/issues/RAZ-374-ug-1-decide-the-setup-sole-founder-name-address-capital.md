# RAZ-374 — UG 1 · Decide the setup: sole founder, name, address, capital

**Tension.** WeDance runs as Alex's Einzelunternehmen (Kleinunternehmer, Nebengewerbe). Ticketing ([RAZ-370](https://linear.app/alosha/issue/RAZ-370/ticketing-for-organizers-sell-passes-on-their-wedance-page-money-to)) means handling organizers' money, live Stripe and contracts. That needs a company with limited liability.

**Setup: Alex founds alone** (Commander, 2026-10-14). Sole shareholder and sole Geschäftsführer.

**Decide (Commander):**

* **Name:** "WeDance UG (haftungsbeschränkt)", the suffix is mandatory. Seat (Munich) and business address: home address or a separate one, since it goes in the public register and the Impressum.
* **Stammkapital:** the legal minimum is €1. In practice enough to cover the first costs (it's working capital, not a fee).
* **Founding document:** the *Musterprotokoll* (standard form) fits a single founder who is also the Geschäftsführer, and is the cheapest route. Own articles (Satzung) are only needed for custom clauses.

**Recommendation:** Musterprotokoll, sole shareholder and Geschäftsführer, a few thousand euros of Stammkapital if cash allows (it covers the first months' costs).

**Next:** [RAZ-375](https://linear.app/alosha/issue/RAZ-375/ug-2-get-ommaxs-written-consent-for-the-wedance-ug-contract-62) (OMMAX consent), [RAZ-371](https://linear.app/alosha/issue/RAZ-371/ug-3-check-the-name-handelsregister-ihk-wedance-trademark) (name check), then [RAZ-376](https://linear.app/alosha/issue/RAZ-376/ug-4-notarised-founding-and-handelsregister-entry-hrb) (notary).

Task: Prepare WeDance UG (haftungsbeschränkt) Company Formation Decision

Context

WeDance currently operates as Alex's Einzelunternehmen (Kleinunternehmer, Nebengewerbe). The upcoming ticketing feature ([RAZ-370](https://linear.app/alosha/issue/RAZ-370/ticketing-for-organizers-sell-passes-on-their-wedance-page-money-to)) involves Stripe payments, festival organiser contracts, ticket proceeds, and refunds.

The project recommendation is to establish WeDance UG (haftungsbeschränkt), with Alex as the sole shareholder and sole Geschäftsführer.

Objectives

1\. Prepare the company formation plan

Document the proposed company structure:

* Company name: WeDance UG (haftungsbeschränkt), subject to name availability and legal review.
* Registered office: Munich, Germany.
* Sole shareholder: Alex.
* Managing director: Alex.
* Founding document: Musterprotokoll, subject to confirmation that the standard formation requirements are satisfied.

2\. Document the outstanding decisions

Identify and clearly flag the following items for Alex's confirmation:

* Whether to use a suitable home address or a separate business address.
* The exact Stammkapital amount, based on a realistic startup budget.
* Confirmation of the company name and registered office.
* Approval to proceed with the proposed formation structure.

Do not invent Alex's address, available funds, legal details, or final decisions.

3\. Prepare a preliminary startup budget

Create a checklist of expected expenses, including:

* Notary and commercial register fees.
* Business registration and administrative costs.
* Accounting, tax advice, and annual reporting.
* Business banking and software.
* Stripe and ticketing operations.
* Refunds and other expected operating expenses.

Distinguish one-time formation expenses from recurring costs. Do not assume share capital is a formation fee or that the minimum legal capital is sufficient working capital.

4\. Outline the formation process

Prepare a proposed checklist covering:

* Company name and address checks.
* Confirmation of share capital and founding document.
* Notarial appointment and execution of the formation documents.
* Opening the appropriate business bank account and paying in the required capital.
* Commercial register application and registration.
* Tax registration, accounting arrangements, and applicable business registrations.
* Review of the legal and payment arrangements required for [RAZ-370](https://linear.app/alosha/issue/RAZ-370/ticketing-for-organizers-sell-passes-on-their-wedance-page-money-to).

5\. Identify legal and operational dependencies

Flag the need to confirm the appropriate contracts, Stripe account ownership, payment flows, refund responsibilities, and handling of organiser funds before ticketing launches.

Clearly distinguish preparation tasks from actions requiring Alex's approval, a notary, an accountant, or a qualified legal professional.

Deliverables

Produce:

1. A company formation decision document.
2. A list of confirmed facts and unresolved decisions.
3. A preliminary startup budget template.
4. A formation checklist with dependencies.
5. A ticketing launch readiness checklist.

Do not register a company, sign documents, make payments, change Stripe ownership, or make binding legal decisions. Prepare the documents and recommendations for Alex to review and approve before proceeding.
