# RAZ-416 — City Battle

**Sketch:** [https://2026.wedance.vip/sketches/battle](<https://2026.wedance.vip/sketches/battle>)
**All sketches:** [https://2026.wedance.vip/sketches](<https://2026.wedance.vip/sketches>)

## Why

The video battle should inspire people to dance, film, discover other dances and cities, meet each other and compete. Today it's a vote box on /cities: one vote → next pair, no result, a leaderboard ranked on 1–5 votes (Barcelona #1 with 1 win), mixed styles per matchup (Madrid Casino vs Budapest Timba), two taps to play, labels colliding with the play/VS buttons on phones. Every approved clip is from July (2026-07) and all checked clips are vertical Shorts shown in 16:9 cards.

## Options

- [ ] **A · Fix it in place** — Keep it on /cities; one tap plays, vote after watching both, show the result, honest standings.
- [ ] **B · Monthly season under Explore** — Battle chip next to Festivals/Artists; one dance per month, timeline, standings, next month's theme + submit.
- [X] **C · Vertical swipe feed** — Full-screen Reels-style: clip A, clip B, pick, see the crowd, next.
- [ ] **D · Home team on the city page** — Your city's live matchup, its champion clip, 'dance with them' events, submit for next month.
- [ ] **E · Recommended: B + D with A's fixes** — Season page under Explore, home-team block on city pages, /cities shrinks to one strip.

**Recommendation:** E — B gives a reason to vote now and to film next month, D uses hometown pride and links clips to real events, A's fixes make voting work. Whatever is picked needs October clips seeded first.
