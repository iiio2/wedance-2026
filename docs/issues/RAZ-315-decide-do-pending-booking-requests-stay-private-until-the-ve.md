# RAZ-315 — Decide: do pending booking requests stay private until the venue accepts?

## Do this

1. Read the recommendation below.
2. Reply in a comment, or move to Done with your answer — Forge picks it up.

## Decision

Recommended: **yes, private until accepted** — a booking becomes a public event only on acceptance. Alternative: show as "requested" on the calendar.

## Why it matters

Today a public, unauthenticated booking form puts a pending request straight onto city feeds (review finding 12).
