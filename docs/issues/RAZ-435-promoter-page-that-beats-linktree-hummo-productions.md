# RAZ-435 — Promoter page that beats Linktree (Hummo Productions)

**Sketch:** [https://2026.wedance.vip/sketches/promoter](<https://2026.wedance.vip/sketches/promoter>) (after the sketch PR merges)

**Tension** — Hummo Productions sends fans to [linktr.ee/hummopro:](<http://linktr.ee/hummopro:>) 10 flat links (a 6-city Kelvis Ochoa tour on Fourvenues, 4 Jamboree shows, broken YouTube/TikTok). Their WeDance page, [2026.wedance.vip/@hummoproductions](<http://2026.wedance.vip/@hummoproductions>), shows 'No upcoming events', so today Linktree wins.

**Driver** — Alösha wants to send Hummo the WeDance link and have them switch on the spot: their artists get their own bookable pages, organizers can go from artist to date to venue, and events stay in sync without Hummo doing anything.

**Options**

* A — Tour-first: the next date as the hero, the tour grouped with a route line, the roster at the bottom
* B — Roster-first: an agency page, each artist with dates and a Book button
* C — Recommended: tabs (Concerts · Artists · Book), plus a promoter view (Fourvenues/Jamboree sync, clicks per city, auto IG stories, booking requests)

**Source of truth (recommended)** — hybrid: shadow-copy from Fourvenues + Jamboree daily, ticket buttons go out to them; claimed fields stop syncing.

**Open questions** — does Hummo manage these artists or only promote the shows? Artist photos needed from Hummo (no stock/AI).
