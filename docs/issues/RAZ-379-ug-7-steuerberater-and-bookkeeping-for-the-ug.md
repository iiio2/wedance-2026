# RAZ-379 — UG 7 · Steuerberater and bookkeeping for the UG

**Tension.** A UG needs double-entry bookkeeping, an annual financial statement (Jahresabschluss, published in the Unternehmensregister), and the § 5a GmbHG reserve: 25% of each year's profit is set aside until the capital reaches €25,000. The current EÜR setup (`ops/finance/`) doesn't cover that.

**Decide (Commander):**

* Steuerberater: who, and the monthly cost.
* Bookkeeping tool: e.g. lexoffice, sevDesk, or the Steuerberater's DATEV.
* Whether `ops/finance/` (finance-daily) also tracks the UG's account. Agent work, once the account exists.

**Blocked by:** none. Choose before the first UG invoice.
