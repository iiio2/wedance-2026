# RAZ-378 — UG 6 · Gewerbeanmeldung, Finanzamt (Steuernummer, USt-IdNr), Transparenzregister

**Tension.** After the register entry, the UG must be known to the trade office, the tax office and the transparency register. Some of this has deadlines.

**Do (Commander, agent prepares the forms):**

1. **Gewerbeanmeldung** for the UG at the KVR München (online). Activity: online platform for dance events, ticketing and event services.
2. **Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung** for the UG via ELSTER, within one month of starting → Steuernummer and USt-IdNr. Decide **Kleinunternehmer or Regelbesteuerung**. With ticket fees and B2B organizers, Regelbesteuerung (charging VAT, reclaiming input VAT) is likely the better choice. Confirm with the Steuerberater.
3. **Transparenzregister:** enter the beneficial owners (shareholders over 25%).
4. **IHK** membership follows automatically. Expect the contribution letter.

**Blocked by:** the HRB number.
