# La Clave Halloween: flyer result

**Best:** `work/v5.png`
**Runner-up:** `work/v3.png`

**Why v5:** all text is proofed correct (Alösha has the ö). The gold "FREE ENTRANCE · FREE WORKSHOPS" banner is the strongest info line. All three faces match their photos and blend into the scene. The Halloween theme sits in the scene (moon, bats, a pile of jack-o'-lanterns) while the people wear their own clothes, and the colours and lettering match the La Clave reference.

**Checklist items it still fails:**
- The teacher lines ("with YUNIOR / with ALÖSHA") end at about 83% of the height, roughly 3% into the bottom 20% that Instagram covers. They are probably still readable above the reply bar, but this is a marginal fail.
- The title starts at about 13% of the height, right at the top limit. This is a minor issue.

## Content decisions
- Title "LA CLAVE" + "Halloween". Date only, because the 9PM–12AM party time sits with the DJ.
- The reason to come is "FREE ENTRANCE · FREE WORKSHOPS" (passes = free entrance; both workshops are free).
- DJ Javier Garcia gets the styles from the brief's music list.
- Both workshops are listed with time, title and teacher.
- Venue: LUMA · Neuhauser Str. 47 · München. The postcode is dropped.
- The "Party" programme line is dropped because it repeats the 21:00 start. Nothing was invented.
